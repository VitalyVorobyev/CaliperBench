# Visible specimen labeling on weld-profile photos

The supplied masks delineate weld regions. They are not outer-object outlines, precise optical edges, or physical dimensions. The left end of some metal specimens is outside the frame, so a complete object diameter or width cannot be recovered from those photos. A border-clipped segment must never be scored as a visible edge.

The review app offers two **proposal** starts for the visible metal silhouette:

1. **Suggest visible silhouette** converts the photo to grayscale, blurs it, applies Otsu's threshold, closes small gaps, retains the largest bright component, and extracts its contour. When the region touches a frame side, the visible contour is an **open edge**. Its endpoints and contacted sides are recorded; the image border is not drawn as an optical edge. If the foreground produces multiple substantial open chains, this single-chain proposal is rejected rather than silently dropping a side; multi-chain review is still needed.
2. **Trace coarse outline** lets a reviewer place sparse points along a visible edge or around the specimen, then choose **Use open edge** or **Close outline**. **Snap to visible edge** interpolates points, searches at most 1–20 pixels along each local normal, and selects a nearby smoothed-image gradient with a distance penalty. Open-edge endpoints remain fixed. Weak-gradient vertices stay in place. The chosen radius is a search bound, not positional uncertainty.

Either route creates an editable local contour. Use the brush to correct corners, reflective areas, internal seams, and background artifacts; undo restores the prior contour. Source weld masks and the original proposal remain separate. An open edge has **no complete object-region mask**. The reviewer can freeze a revision for local investigation, but the app does not export these weld images as benchmark references.

The next geometry stage needs a separate specimen **region mask** for the interior and reviewed **visible boundary chains** for optical edges. Support multiple chains per image before centerline evaluation. Derive a provisional centerline from the mask's distance transform or medial axis, then trim branches and a border buffer near clipped ends. Estimate its local tangent over a neighborhood; orthogonal sections are candidates only where both sides intersect distinct reviewed visible chains, remain inside the image, and avoid ambiguous transitions. If one side is missing, mark that section ineligible rather than extrapolating a width. The caliper then refines the two eligible crossings; it does not decide which mask boundary is real.

For a later **edge-localization** subset, define which visible outer-boundary segments are in scope, exclude image-border closures and ambiguous/internal material transitions, collect independent reviewer corrections with uncertainty, and derive caliper scans normal to those accepted segments. For **physical-size** ground truth, use another dataset with a traceable measurement and calibration, such as the apple fruitlet candidate; a plausible image contour alone does not provide it.
